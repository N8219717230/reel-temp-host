# Ramayan series: 15 parts (one per day). Valmiki Ramayana is the source; Britannica 'Ramayana' for cross-checking.
Each part = ~30-35 s, 8-11 cuts, ~60-75 spoken Hindi words. Last line is always a teaser ending with «अगले भाग में।» (Part 15 ends with a closing line instead).
Tell the story with objects, places and consequences: NO battles, blood or weapons being used on people. Respectful tone: this is a sacred epic.

1. Ayodhya, King Dasharatha, the Putrakameshti yajna, birth of Rama, Bharata, Lakshmana, Shatrughna. (DONE)
2. Sage Vishwamitra asks for Rama and Lakshmana; the forest of Tadaka freed from fear (forest turns green again); Ahalya freed from her curse; they reach Mithila.
3. King Janaka's Mithila, Shiva's great bow, Sita's swayamvara, Rama and the bow, the four royal weddings (Rama-Sita, Lakshmana-Urmila, Bharata-Mandavi, Shatrughna-Shrutakirti).
4. Dasharatha decides to crown Rama; Manthara and Kaikeyi; the two boons; Rama must go to the forest for fourteen years, Bharata to be king.
5. Rama, Sita and Lakshmana leave Ayodhya; Guha and the Ganga; Chitrakoot; Dasharatha's passing; Bharata refuses the throne and carries Rama's sandals (paduka) home.
6. Dandaka forest and the sages; Panchavati; Shurpanakha's visit and its consequence (told gently: she leaves angry).
7. Ravana's plan; Maricha as the golden deer; Lakshmana's line (rekha); Sita is carried away in the Pushpaka vimana.
8. Jatayu shows the way; Kabandha; Shabari's berries; Rama and Lakshmana reach the Pampa lake.
9. Hanuman meets Rama and Lakshmana on Rishyamukha; friendship with Sugriva; the promise to search for Sita.
10. Sugriva and Vali (told through the kingdom, not the fight); Sugriva becomes king; search parties set out in four directions.
11. Sampati sees Lanka; Hanuman's great leap over the ocean; Ashoka Vatika; Sita and the ring; Lanka's fire (Hanuman's tail lamp); Hanuman returns with Sita's jewel.
12. Vibhishana joins Rama; the ocean is asked for a path; Nala and Nila build Ram Setu; the army crosses to Lanka.
13. The war begins (shown as armies on a map and torches); Angada's message; Lakshmana wounded; Hanuman brings the Sanjeevani mountain.
14. Kumbhakarna and Indrajit fall; Ravana's end (shown as the ten-headed crown falling, no violence); Sita and Rama reunited; Vibhishana crowned.
15. Fourteen years done: the return in Pushpaka vimana, Ayodhya lit with lamps (Diwali), Bharata and the sandals, Rama's coronation, Ram Rajya. Closing line, no 'next part'.

Character colours (keep identical in every part): Rama = royal blue robe, Lakshmana = wine, Bharata = green, Shatrughna = orange-brown, Sita = queen figure in blue/gold, Dasharatha = king figure, sages = 'scholar' figure (green). Figures have no faces. Hanuman: orange robe, tail drawn as a paper flame when needed.
